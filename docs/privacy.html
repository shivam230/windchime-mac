Windchime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Windchime does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no network connections.

Microphone

Windchime can listen to your microphone so that blowing toward your Mac rings the chimes. This is entirely optional and off by default — it only turns on when you tap the microphone button.

When it is on, audio is analysed live on your device to estimate how hard you are blowing, and is then immediately discarded. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Data we store on your device

The only thing Windchime saves is the widget's window position and your chosen settings (wind level, volume), kept locally in macOS UserDefaults so the widget reopens the way you left it. This never leaves your Mac.

Third parties

None. Windchime uses no third-party SDKs, advertising, or services.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/shivam230/windchime-mac.

Windchime is a free, open desktop widget. This policy applies to the macOS app.

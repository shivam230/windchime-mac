## Description (≤4000)
Windchime is a tiny, beautiful windchime that lives on your desktop and brings a moment of calm to your day.

It floats quietly above your work and sways in a gentle, ever-changing breeze — ringing softly on its own, never demanding your attention. When you need a moment, lean in and blow toward your Mac's microphone and the chimes answer your breath, just like a real windchime catching the wind. Or brush your cursor across the bells to play them yourself.

The sound is warm and real — every note is synthesized live from the tones of Himalayan brass bells, so it never loops or repeats. Three wind settings let you choose the mood, from a slow, meditative calm to a livelier breeze.

FEATURES
• A transparent, frameless chime that floats on your desktop
• Rings on its own in a continuous, gently-shifting breeze
• Blow toward your microphone to make it ring — audio is analysed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere
• Brush or click the bells to play them by hand
• Three wind moods: calm, breeze, and windy
• Adjustable volume, an always-on-top pin, and a resizable, movable window
• Optional "Open at Login" so it's there whenever you start your Mac
• Tiny and light — a few megabytes, and easy on the battery

PRIVACY
Windchime collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no network connections. The microphone is optional, off by default, and any audio is processed live and immediately discarded.

Let a little calm live in the corner of your screen.
